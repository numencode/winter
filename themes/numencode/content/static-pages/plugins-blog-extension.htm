NumenCode.BlogExtension extends Winter.Blog with what most blogs need sooner or later:

  • Tags with translatable names and URLs, and the menu items \"Blog tag\" and \"All blog tags\".
  • A picture gallery and attached files for every post – publishable one by one.
  • Bricks for posts, when NumenCode.Bricks is installed.
  • Components: tag list, post tags, tag page, related posts, breadcrumbs, reading time and an RSS feed.
  • A Faker page for developers that fills a blog with demo posts.
